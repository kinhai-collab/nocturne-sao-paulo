Choose your next great dinner.

Choose up to five restaurants. Open the floating Your ballot ticket to review your selections, sign in with Google, then submit.

Your choices stay on this device before you sign in. Signing in keeps those choices; submitting saves your ballot to the organizer’s private Supabase database.

A fair, private poll

Each restaurant you choose receives one vote. Choices are unranked. One ballot is accepted per Google account, and retrying the same ballot does not create a duplicate.

Your verified name, email and choices are visible only to the organizer. Public results show totals. A vote is not a reservation.

Company budget

The guide is US$125 per person. Each price names the menu being compared; drinks, service and exchange fees can increase the total. Open restaurant details for other menus, original BRL prices and sources.

Having trouble connecting?

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, voting runs on Supabase, and sign-in uses Google. A work network may restrict any of these services. Your draft remains on this device while you retry. Contact the organizer if the connection still fails.
