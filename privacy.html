Privacy & data use

Last updated November 13, 2026. Questions or deletion requests: kinminghai@gmail.com.

Restaurant voting

Enkai uses Google sign-in to identify voters and accept one ballot per Google account. Each ballot can contain up to five restaurants.

The poll organizer stores your verified Google account identifier, name, email address, voting time and selected restaurants in a private Supabase database. your first initial appears in the public participation list. Public results show restaurant totals; the public results endpoint does not return voter names or email addresses.

Google sign-in tokens are used to verify your account and are not stored in the vote database. Your browser stores your shortlist and a local copy of your submitted ballot. Google provides sign-in, Supabase processes and stores voting records, and GitHub Pages hosts the website.

For questions about your voting record or to request its removal, contact the organizer who invited you to this poll.

Using the poll

This is an informal dining preference poll. A selection is not a booking, payment or reservation. Restaurant booking links take you to external websites. One ballot is saved per Google account. Sign in again to load your saved choices, edit them and save changes.

Meal planner privacy

Supabase stores meal plans, responses, Google or Microsoft account identifiers and guest display names. The organizer can see each guest’s display name and choices. Guests can see the meal, their own poll response, and attendee names with RSVP status. Individual poll choices are visible to the organizer. Anyone who receives a forwarded invite link can join with Google or a personal Microsoft account. Linking requires signing in to both accounts. The planner stores your account email address to deliver dinner invitations. Calendar access is separate from signing in. Saved guest names, restaurant wish lists, interest notes and manually entered calendar blocks stay on this device. These local lists and manual blocks are not uploaded to Google or Outlook. Anyone with an invitation link can preview the title, destination, chosen restaurant and time, and the host’s first name before signing in; other guests and their responses are not included in this preview.

By default, a sign-in token stays in this tab for up to 24 hours so refreshing does not sign you out. Choosing Keep me signed in stores it in this browser for up to 30 days. The database stores only a hash of this token. Sign out revokes the saved session. Unfinished plans and responses stay in this tab until you save them or sign out.

Optional calendar connections

If you connect Google or Outlook, Enkai checks your Google primary calendar or Outlook default calendar for overlapping event titles and times. Conflict details are shown only to you, are not shared with dinner organizers or other guests, and are not saved in the Enkai database. Enkai stores your calendar account email, provider identifier, connection status, and encrypted refresh credential in private server storage. It uses that permission to create and update dinner invitations from the organizer’s calendar, and to accept, tentatively accept or decline your invitation when you explicitly confirm your response for a finalized date. A poll vote alone does not accept an invitation.

Calendar invitations reveal the organizer and attendee email addresses through the calendar provider. Invitations and cancellation notices are sent by Google or Microsoft. Enkai stores event identifiers and sync status to avoid duplicates and retry delayed deliveries. Disconnect in Settings to remove Enkai’s stored calendar credential; existing calendar events remain. You may also revoke access in your Google or Microsoft account settings. Without calendar permission you can still vote and RSVP, then respond to an invitation in email. Changes made directly in your calendar are flagged for review rather than silently overwritten.

Google API data use

Enkai’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google calendar data is used only to provide the calendar features described here. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Live restaurant search sends the city and food type you enter to Google Maps. See the Google Maps Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
