Voting privacy

Nocturne uses Google sign-in to identify voters and accept one ballot per Google account. Each ballot can contain up to five restaurants.

The poll organizer stores your verified Google account identifier, name, email address, voting time and selected restaurants in a private Supabase database. For new ballots, your first initial appears in the public participation list. Public results show restaurant totals; the public results endpoint does not return voter names or email addresses.

Google sign-in tokens are used to verify your account and are not stored in the vote database. Your browser stores your shortlist and a local copy of your submitted ballot. Google provides sign-in, Supabase processes and stores voting records, and GitHub Pages hosts the website.

For questions about your voting record or to request its removal, contact the organizer who invited you to this poll.

Using the poll

This is an informal dining preference poll. A selection is not a booking, payment or reservation. Restaurant booking links take you to external websites. One submitted ballot is accepted per Google account; contact the organizer if you need a correction.
