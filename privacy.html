Voting privacy

Nocturne uses Google sign-in to identify voters and accept one ballot per Google account. Each ballot can contain up to five restaurants.

The poll organizer stores your verified Google account identifier, name, email address, voting time and selected restaurants in a private Supabase database. your first initial appears in the public participation list. Public results show restaurant totals; the public results endpoint does not return voter names or email addresses.

Google sign-in tokens are used to verify your account and are not stored in the vote database. Your browser stores your shortlist and a local copy of your submitted ballot. Google provides sign-in, Supabase processes and stores voting records, and GitHub Pages hosts the website.

For questions about your voting record or to request its removal, contact the organizer who invited you to this poll.

Using the poll

This is an informal dining preference poll. A selection is not a booking, payment or reservation. Restaurant booking links take you to external websites. One ballot is saved per Google account. Sign in again to load your saved choices, edit them and save changes.

Meal planner privacy

Supabase stores meal plans, responses, Google or Microsoft account identifiers and guest display names. The organizer can see each guest’s display name and choices. Guests can see the meal and their own response. Anyone who receives a forwarded invite link can join with Google or a personal Microsoft account. Linking requires signing in to both accounts. The planner does not save your Google email address or sign-in token. Manually entered calendar blocks stay in this browser.

Remember me stores a sign-in token in this browser for up to 30 days. Sign out revokes that saved session.

Live restaurant search sends the city and food type you enter to Google Maps. See the Google Maps Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
